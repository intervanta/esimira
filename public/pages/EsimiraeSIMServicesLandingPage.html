FAQ

Everything You Need to Know About Our Services

What is an eSIM, and how is it different from a traditional SIM card?

×

An eSIM, or embedded SIM, digitally stores your mobile network information on compatible devices. Unlike traditional SIM cards, it doesn't need to be physically inserted or swapped when changing phones. Instead, you can easily activate your new device's eSIM for instant connectivity without using a physical card.

How easily can you switch between multiple SIM profiles?

+

Switching between SIM profiles is incredibly easy with eSIM technology. You can manage multiple profiles through your device settings without any physical SIM card swapping.

How convenient is it to switch between multiple SIM profiles?

+

It's extremely convenient! You can switch between different carriers and plans instantly through your device settings, making it perfect for international travel.

How many eSIMs can my phone store?

+

Most modern smartphones can store between 5-20 eSIM profiles, depending on the device model. You can have multiple profiles but typically use only one or two at a time.

When does the validity period of my eSIM begin?

+

The validity period typically begins when you first connect to a mobile network using your eSIM, not when you purchase or install it.

I have deleted my eSIM. Could you please issue a new one or process a refund?

+

If you accidentally delete your eSIM, please contact our support team. We can help restore access or provide a replacement based on our terms and conditions.

Which payment methods are accepted?

+

We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and various digital payment methods for your convenience.
